Frequently asked questions

Are these physical books?

No. Products in this shop are digital PDF files. Nothing will be shipped to your address.

How do I receive my PDF?

After your payment is confirmed, the seller will send the PDF to the email address or WhatsApp contact you provide.

Can I get a refund?

Please ask the seller about the refund policy before paying. The seller should publish a clear policy that follows applicable consumer-protection rules.

Is checkout automatic?

No. This starter website uses manual order requests and payment confirmation. It does not collect card details or automatically deliver files.
